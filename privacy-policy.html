Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 22, 2024

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, make a purchase, or communicate with us. This may include your name, email address, phone number, and payment information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and improve our services, process transactions, and send you technical notices, updates, and promotional messages.

3. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect information about you from loss, theft, misuse, and unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.

4. Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience on our website. You can choose to set your web browser to refuse cookies, but some parts of the site may not function properly.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.

6. Personal Information

This includes details such as your full name, email address, phone number, billing and shipping address, and payment information that you provide when placing an order, creating an account, or contacting customer support.

7. Business Information

If you interact with us for partnership, wholesale, or commercial purposes, we may collect business-related details such as company name, business type, tax information, transaction records, and other relevant operational data necessary to provide our services.

8. Usage Information

We automatically collect certain technical data when you visit our website. This may include your IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, pages visited, time spent on the site, and other analytics data that help us improve performance, security, and user experience.

9. Text / SMS Marketing

Consent to Receive Messages

By entering your phone number on our website, subscribing to updates, or completing a purchase, you consent to receive SMS communications from Trend Crafters. These communications may include promotional messages, special discounts, product launches, restock alerts, and important service notifications related to your orders. Your consent to receive marketing messages is not a condition of purchase.

Types of Messages We Send

Our SMS communications may include order confirmations, shipping updates, delivery reminders, customer service follow-ups, promotional campaigns, limited-time offers, and exclusive member deals. Message frequency may vary depending on your interaction with our brand, ongoing promotions, and subscription preferences.

Automated Technology Disclosure

Opt-Out & Unsubscribe Policy

You may opt out of SMS marketing at any time by replying "STOP" to any text message received from us. Once you opt out, you will receive a confirmation message, and promotional texts will cease. However, transactional or service-related messages (such as order updates) may still be sent when necessary to complete your purchase or provide customer support.

10. Retention Period

Trend Crafters retains your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, including processing orders, providing customer support, complying with legal obligations, resolving disputes, and enforcing our agreements.

11. Right to Update

Trend Crafters reserves the right to update, modify, or revise this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect changes in our business practices, legal requirements, or service offerings. Any updates will be effective immediately upon being posted on our website unless otherwise stated.

12. Order Processing & Fulfillment

We use your personal information to process, confirm, and deliver your orders efficiently. This includes managing payments, coordinating shipping, sending order updates, and providing customer support related to your purchases.
