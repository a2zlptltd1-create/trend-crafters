# Trend Crafters - Professional Image Prompts

This document contains curated image prompts designed to enhance the professional and premium feel of the **Trend Crafters** wholesale apparel website.

## 1. Hero & Header Images
*Goal: Create a stunning first impression with high-end, clean, and professional visuals.*

- **Prompt 1 (Minimalist Lifestyle):** 
  > A high-end fashion studio shot of a minimalist clothing rack featuring premium navy blue and charcoal grey shirts, soft cinematic lighting, yellow accent decor in the background, 8k resolution, professional commercial photography, clean white background.
- **Prompt 2 (Wholesale/B2B Feel):** 
  > A luxury apparel showroom with neatly stacked high-quality t-shirts and denim jeans, soft overhead lighting, premium textures visible, 4k, photorealistic, depth of field, professional retail interior design.
- **Prompt 3 (Abstract Fashion):** 
  > Close-up macro shot of high-quality fabric texture (cotton and denim) with a subtle navy blue and yellow color palette, soft shadows, premium feel, architectural composition, high-end fashion magazine style.

---

## 2. Category Images
*Goal: Clear, professional, and consistent visuals for navigation cards.*

- **Shirts Category:**
  > Professional studio product photography of a premium button-down shirt and a t-shirt, folded neatly on a marble surface, soft natural lighting, high-end fashion catalog style, 8k.
- **Pants Category:**
  > Close-up of high-quality denim jeans and chinos, focusing on the stitching and fabric quality, neutral studio background, professional lighting, sharp details.
- **Shoes Category:**
  > Sleek modern sneakers and leather boots on a clean podium, minimalist aesthetic, side profile view, soft shadows, luxury footwear photography.
- **Caps/Accessories Category:**
  > A collection of premium snapback caps and beanies arranged artistically, high-quality embroidery details visible, professional studio lighting, clean aesthetic.
- **Denim Collection:**
  > High-quality dark wash denim jeans, neatly folded, focus on copper rivets and orange contrast stitching, professional textile photography, minimalist background.
- **Premium Outerwear (Jackets):**
  > A premium black leather biker jacket on a minimalist stand, soft grain texture visible, high-end fashion lighting, deep blacks and rich shadows, 8k resolution.
- **Sunglasses & Accessories:**
  > Modern designer sunglasses on a reflective glass surface, soft sunlight reflection, cinematic depth of field, luxury lifestyle aesthetic, high-end commercial shot.

## 3. Product & Feature Shots
*Goal: Focus on quality, durability, and craftsmanship.*

- **Urban Essential T-Shirt:**
  > A ghost mannequin shot of a premium black t-shirt, perfectly fitted, visible cotton grain, professional studio lighting, isolated on a light grey background.
- **Velocity Pro Sneakers:**
  > Dynamic action shot of modern sneakers, floating in mid-air with a slight motion blur, high-contrast lighting, highlighting the sole and texture, professional sports brand style.
- **Fabric Detail (Trust Metric):**
  > Extreme macro shot of fabric weaving, showing durability and high-end material, soft cinematic lighting, professional textile photography.

## 4. Lifestyle & Model Shots
*Goal: Show the apparel in use to build brand identity.*

- **Wholesale Team/Support:**
  > A professional and friendly customer support team working in a modern, brightly lit office with navy blue and yellow branding elements, blurred background, high-end corporate photography.
- **Fashion Model (Male/Female):**
  > A stylish model wearing premium streetwear (navy hoodie and denim), walking in a modern urban environment during golden hour, soft sunlight, high-end fashion lookbook style.
- **Wholesale Warehouse (Logistics):**
  > A wide shot of a modern, clean, and organized clothing warehouse with rows of high-quality apparel on hangers, warm lighting, professional logistics environment, 4k.

---

## Tips for Best Results:
* **Consistency:** Use the same "lighting style" across all prompts (e.g., "soft cinematic lighting" or "bright studio lighting").
* **Aspect Ratio:** If using Midjourney, add `--ar 16:9` for headers or `--ar 1:1` for product cards.
* **Colors:** Always mention **Navy Blue** and **Yellow** as secondary or accent colors to maintain brand harmony.
